# Aphotic Orb look mechanics

The orb keeps its central shell, scale, and floating anchor fixed. Direction is attention, not turntable rotation. The hexagonal aperture and hard-edged core aim first; embedded filaments brighten toward the target; attached fins compress behind and open slightly toward the target. The graphite shell stays front-readable and does not spin.

The emblem-inspired aperture keeps its construction and its small spark on the original side. Luminous shell band, central core, and circuit filaments remain separate clean theme regions.

- `000 up`: core shifts inside the aperture toward the top, upper filaments brighten, lower attached fins open slightly.
- `090 screen-right`: aperture and core aim toward the image-right edge, right filaments brighten, left attached fins compress.
- `180 down`: core shifts toward the bottom, lower filaments brighten, upper fins open slightly.
- `270 screen-left`: aperture and core aim toward the image-left edge, left filaments brighten, right attached fins compress.

Each 22.5-degree step moves the aperture aim, filament emphasis, and fin compression evenly. Never rotate, skew, or relocate the entire orb. No detached light, rays, particles, aura, or soft glow.
